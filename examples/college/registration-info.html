Registration

The College’s student records system allows returning students the convenience of registering completely online.

New students

New students must complete the application process and initial advisement before registering. Apply early for the best selection of classes.

Returning students (within four terms)

Make an appointment with your program advisor to schedule your classes. Remember to register early for the best selection of courses.

Students who have not attended for more than four consecutive terms

Complete a readmission form and return it to the Admissions Office. Meet with an academic counselor to access your Student Information System, verify program requirements, arrange a schedule and register.

Follow These Easy Steps to Register Online
Register – MyBlazeMeter Student Information System
  1. Click the button above to go to the MyBlazeMeter Student Information System.
  2. Click on Register or Drop Classes.
  3. Select a term and click Continue.
  4. Enter the Subject, Course Number, and Campus, then click Search.
  5. Review your search results and click Add on the right to add the course to your cart (to add another course, click Search again).
  6. Make sure to click Submit in the summary pane to move the class from Pending status to Registered status.
Follow These Easy Steps to Register Online from a Plan
  1. Go to the MyBlazeMeter Student Information System and click Register or Drop Classes.
  2. Select a term and click Continue.
  3. Go to the Plans tab.
  4. Click Add to add sections individually, or Add All to add every planned course section to your cart.
  5. Click Submit in the summary pane to move the classes from Pending to Registered status.
