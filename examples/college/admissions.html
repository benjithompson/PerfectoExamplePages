BlazeMeter Tech offers:

  • Support for Your Success – Faculty and staff are dedicated to inspiring students to achieve personal fulfillment and personal success.
  • Educational Excellence – Both credit and non-credit courses are taught by accomplished and knowledgeable faculty.
  • Convenience – Four campus locations in Brookdale, Ashford, Clearview, and Northgate make commuting easy.
  • Flexibility – In addition to on-site classes, distance learning opportunities such as online and hybrid courses are offered.

Five steps for admission to our credit course programs

BlazeMeter Tech has an open-door admissions policy limited only by the following criteria: a student must be a high school graduate or the equivalent, or at least eighteen years of age and able to benefit from instruction.

Step 1: Submit an Application

Submit an Application to the College:

  • For fast, easy admission, apply online or download and complete the paper application.
  • Review details about the College’s admissions policy and procedures.
Step 2: Proof of Residency

Residency status is determined when a student first registers at the College and when reentering after an absence.

To submit proof of Residency:

  • Complete the Declaration of Residency for Tuition form found on the MyBlazeMeter/Student Information System portal (access is available once you complete the application process).

Provide Proof of Lakeland Residency (for those seeking in-state tuition):

  • Present your Lakeland driver’s license or State I.D.

Please Note: To be considered an in-state student for tuition purposes, you must submit your Lakeland proof of residency on or before registering for classes.

Step 3: Educational Financing

Plan Your Educational Financing:

Review the college’s tuition and fees. Students are encouraged to apply for federal financial aid (FAFSA) no later than April 1 and submit all documents by May 1 for the fall semester.

  • Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).
  • BlazeMeter Promise Scholarship.
  • Visit our Financial Aid Office.
Step 4: Course Placement

1. Determine if Language Support is Needed:

Students seeking degrees, diplomas, or credit certificates must provide evidence of English language proficiency.

Determine Language Placement

2. Determine Math and English Placement:

The College applies the multiple measures approach to determine placement for math and English.

Determine Your English Placement Determine Your Math Placement
Step 5: Initial Advisement

Meet with an academic counselor for REQUIRED initial advisement. If you have questions regarding registration or financial aid, please contact student support.
